Word mastery — spaced repetition

      
Words you get right several times in a row come back less and less often (1 day, then 3, 7, 14, 30 days). Words you miss come back soon. The Meaning Quiz and Listen & Spell mostly use words that are due or still improving, with a few mastered words mixed in to check they are remembered.

    Oral Partner settings
    
      
    How the Oral Partner mock exam works for you.
    
      
    
        
        
        
      
    
      
    Answer recordings are saved in your account so you can listen back. Recordings older than 7 days are deleted automatically when you sign in. Written answers and scores are kept.
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
      
    Helpful phrases are shown only during preparation, never while answering — just like the real exam. In a mock exam the examiner stays silent like a real examiner; in practice mode the examiner asks one gentle follow-up when an answer is very short.
