PSLE Reading Aloud — marking requirements (from the Your English Genie Oral Pack, 2025):

1. Pronunciation — pronounce the ending sounds of every word clearly (e.g. /s/, /d/, /t/, /k/, /ch/ — say 'gift', not 'gif'). Pronounce 'the' as /thee/ before a vowel sound. Practise difficult sounds such as /th/ at home.

2. Intonation — change your voice to show feelings, themes and attitudes. Your voice goes up slightly at the end of questions and for exclamation marks. Match your tone to the theme of the passage (lively and energetic for a fun school event). Change your voice for each character's dialogue — an angry speaker sounds lower, an excited speaker sounds higher.

3. Pace and pausing — do not rush; this is not a contest to find the fastest reader. Read at a conversational pace, or slightly slower. Pause slightly (less than 1 second) at full stops, commas, exclamation marks and question marks. For very long sentences, pause slightly in the middle where the phrasing allows.

4. Volume — not too loud like a loudspeaker, and not mumbling softly like a mouse. Speak at a clear, steady volume, as if talking to someone across a table.

5. Fluency vs accuracy — when you meet an unfamiliar word, keep going. Do not stop, stumble or pause for a long time. Sound confident even if you are unsure of a word — secure the fluency marks rather than losing them by stopping.

Marking: give a mark out of 10, with strengths and what to improve, based on these five areas.
