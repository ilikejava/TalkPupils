Your words

      

    

    

      
Word mastery — spaced repetition

      
Words you get right several times in a row come back less and less often (1 day, then 3, 7, 14, 30 days). Words you miss come back soon. The Meaning Quiz and Listen & Spell mostly use words that are due or still improving, with a few mastered words mixed in to check they are remembered.

    Oral Partner settings
    
      
    How the Oral Partner mock exam works for your child.
    
      
    
        
        
        
      
    
      
    Answer recordings are kept for 7 days, then deleted automatically to save space. Your child's written answers and scores are kept.
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
      
    Helpful phrases are shown only during preparation, never while answering — just like the real exam. In a mock exam the examiner stays silent like a real examiner; in practice mode the examiner asks one gentle follow-up when an answer is very short. Your pictures and recorded answers live in the app folder (data/stimuli and data/audio). They are not included in backup downloads — copy the whole app folder to back them up.

    AI marking (optional)
    
      
    If you switch this on, after each reading test the app also asks an AI (ChatGPT or Claude) to mark the reading and give feedback in simple words. It is optional — without it, the local mark is used. You need your own API key from the AI provider (pay-per-use, a few cents per reading). The same key is also used for Oral Partner feedback (a refined answer and an SBC mark) when this is switched on.
    
      
    
        How to get an API key (tap to read)
        
    ChatGPT (OpenAI): 1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign in (or create a free account). 2. Click your profile icon (top right) → “API keys”. 3. Click “Create new secret key”, give it a name, and copy the key — it starts with sk-. Keep it secret; anyone with it can spend your credit. 4. You may need to add a little credit first (Billing → Add payment method). The first readings cost only a few cents each.
    
        
    Claude (Anthropic): 1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in (or create a free account). 2. On the left, click “API Keys”. 3. Click “Create Key”, give it a name, and copy the key — it starts with sk-ant-. 4. You may need to add a little credit first (Settings → Billing). The first readings cost only a few cents each.
    
        
    Then paste the key in the box below, choose the provider, tick “Use AI marking”, and click “Save AI settings”. Use “Test connection” to check the key works before your child practises.
    
      
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
      
    
      
    
        
        
        
      
    
      
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
      
    
      
    Privacy: when AI marking is on, the passage text and what the microphone heard are sent to the AI provider you chose (OpenAI or Anthropic). For Oral Partner, the examiner's question and the child's spoken answer (as text) are sent. No audio is ever sent. The key is stored only on this laptop, in the data folder, and is never included in backup downloads.
